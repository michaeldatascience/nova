How long does shipping take?

Orders dispatch within 48 hours. Standard delivery is 3–7 business days, free over $100.

What is the return policy?

30-day, no-questions returns on unused products. Every device carries a 12-month warranty.

Is the ring waterproof?

Yes — 5ATM (50 m) waterproofing on all rings; IP68 on bands. Shower, swim and train with it.

Are these medical devices?

They are wellness and screening devices, not diagnostic tools. The Sleep Belt 1657B is a Class I medical instrument. Always consult a physician for medical decisions.
