Our story

From clinical factory floor to your finger

NOVA products are engineered in ISO 13485 certified facilities by a team that has shipped medical-grade wearables to more than a thousand health-tech brands, hospitals and insurance programs around the world.

That heritage shows up in the details: multi-wavelength PPG arrays validated against clinical gold standards, algorithms with >95% accuracy in heart rate and sleep staging, and titanium bodies built for 24/7 wear.

  • Clinically validated against PSG and ECG gold standards
  • Medical-grade SpO₂, ECG and temperature sensing
  • Data encrypted in transit, GDPR-aligned privacy
